# 7. What is coming

Some of what the franchise program describes is live today, and some is still being built. This
page is honest about which is which. Anything not live yet is simply left out of your dashboard,
never shown as zero, because a zero would claim something about your business that we cannot yet
back up.

## Live today

| Program promise | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Your terms, territory, instalments and signed agreement | Overview, Your record |
| Machine-wise sales, shake volume and gross revenue | Statistics, Orders |
| Machine status | Machines, Remote control, Logs |
| Stock levels and restocking | Machine, Stock |
| Customer feedback | Feedback |

## Coming

These need MBP's monthly settlement, which is still being built. They appear on the bottom of your
Overview as **Coming**.

| Report | What it will show |
| --- | --- |
| Protein and cup consumption | How much protein and how many cups your machines used |
| Operating costs | Transportation, warehousing and the other costs your agreement lets you deduct |
| Advertising income | Your share of advertising on your machines |
| Distributable profit | Profit after the costs your agreement allows |
| Payouts to you | What has been paid to you, and when |
| Capital recovered so far | How much of your capital you have recovered, and how much remains |
| Monthly statements | A statement for each month |
| Alerts | Operational alerts, such as a machine that needs attention |

None of these changes what your agreement entitles you to. They make it visible.

## Availability on your account

The Overview and Your record are available to every active franchise. The machine pages (Machines,
Statistics, Orders, Logs, Feedback and the rest) and operator logins are switched on for each
franchise by MBP as they are rolled out, so you may not see every page yet. If a page described in
this guide is missing from your menu, ask the MuscleBoxPro franchise team.

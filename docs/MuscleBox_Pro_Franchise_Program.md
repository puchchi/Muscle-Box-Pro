# MuscleBox Pro Franchise Program

## Build and Operate a MuscleBox Pro Network

MuscleBox Pro is building a technology-enabled network of automated protein shake machines across gyms and fitness centers.

The MuscleBox Pro Franchise Program allows selected partners to develop and operate a defined market while benefiting from the MuscleBox Pro technology platform, machine infrastructure, protein supply pipeline, centralized payment systems, advertising network, and established gym partnership model.

The franchisee invests in the expansion of the MuscleBox Pro network and participates in the profits generated by the machines assigned to their franchise.

# 17. Capital Recovery Model

The Territory Franchise operates under a capital recovery model.

Until the franchisee has received cumulative eligible protein-business profit of **₹25,00,000**:

## Protein Business

**100% of applicable MuscleBox Pro distributable protein-business profit is allocated to the franchisee.**

This continues until the franchisee has recovered the full ₹25,00,000 franchise investment through eligible protein-business profit distributions.

---

# 18. Advertising Revenue

Advertising is treated separately from the capital recovery mechanism.

Advertising income does **not** count toward the franchisee's ₹25,00,000 capital recovery.

### Advertising Profit Share

**25% — Franchisee**

**75% — MuscleBox Pro**

This advertising participation continues after the franchisee completes capital recovery, subject to the applicable advertising terms.

Advertising profit will be calculated after applicable advertising-related costs and expenses.

---

# 19. After Capital Recovery

Once the franchisee has received cumulative eligible protein-business profit of ₹25,00,000, the franchise moves into the post-recovery phase.

## Protein Business

**50% — Franchisee**

**50% — MuscleBox Pro**

## Advertising

**25% — Franchisee**

**75% — MuscleBox Pro**

The franchisee therefore continues to participate in the long-term economics of the MuscleBox Pro network even after recovering the initial franchise investment.

---

# 20. Capital Recovery Example

For illustration:

If the franchisee has received:

**₹20,00,000**

in eligible protein-business profit distributions:

**Remaining recovery amount = ₹5,00,000**

Any advertising income received during this period does not reduce the ₹5,00,000 remaining recovery amount.

If the franchisee subsequently receives ₹8,00,000 in eligible protein-business profit:

- ₹5,00,000 completes capital recovery.
- The remaining ₹3,00,000 is distributed under the post-recovery 50:50 protein profit-sharing model.

The MuscleBox Pro dashboard will track the recovery status.

# 28. Franchisee Operational Responsibilities

The franchisee is responsible for the day-to-day operation and upkeep of all MuscleBox Pro machines assigned to its franchise.

The franchisee must ensure that machines remain operational, adequately stocked and available for customers during the agreed operating hours of each gym.

### The franchisee is specifically responsible for:

- Regularly checking the operational status of each machine
- Monitoring protein and consumable inventory
- Replenishing protein powder when inventory reaches the required refill level
- Refilling cups and other approved consumables
- Ensuring sufficient stock is available at each machine
- Coordinating local transportation of protein and consumables
- Moving protein and consumables from the franchisee warehouse to individual machine locations
- Maintaining appropriate storage conditions for protein and consumables
- Monitoring expiry dates and preventing the use of expired products
- Keeping the machine and its operating area reasonably clean
- Coordinating routine local operational requirements
- Responding to machine or inventory alerts through the MuscleBox Pro platform
- Reporting technical issues to MuscleBox Pro promptly
- Coordinating access to the machine with the gym
- Ensuring machines are not unnecessarily left non-operational

## Responsibility Cannot Be Shifted to the Gym

The franchisee's operational responsibilities are an essential part of the franchise model.

The franchisee **may not transfer or delegate the responsibility for stocking, refilling, maintaining inventory or managing the day-to-day operation of MuscleBox Pro machines to the gym owner, gym employees or other third parties without prior approval from MuscleBox Pro.**

The gym may provide reasonable access and cooperation for machine operation, but the franchisee remains responsible for ensuring that the machine is stocked and operational.

MuscleBox Pro may, at its discretion, approve a different operating arrangement for a particular location.

Any such arrangement must be expressly approved by MuscleBox Pro and will not automatically apply to other franchise locations.

## Operational Performance

Failure to maintain adequate stock or operational availability may be considered a performance issue under the franchise agreement.

Repeated failure to maintain machines in an operational and adequately stocked condition may affect:

- Franchise performance status
- Territorial exclusivity
- Eligibility for additional machines
- Expansion opportunities
- Other franchise rights

The detailed service-level and performance requirements will be defined in the definitive franchise agreement.

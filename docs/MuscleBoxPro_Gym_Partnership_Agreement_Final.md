### 6. PROFIT-SHARING MODEL

6.1 **Initial ratio.** Until the Milestone (defined below) is reached, Net Profit from protein shake sales shall be shared **80% MuscleBoxPro : 20% Gym**.

6.2 **Post-Milestone ratio.** From the point the Milestone is reached, Net Profit from all subsequent protein shake sales shall be shared **50% MuscleBoxPro : 50% Gym** for the remainder of the Term.

6.3 **The Milestone** is reached on the earlier of:
(a) 15,000 cumulative completed paid cups sold through the Machine; or
(b) ₹5,00,000 cumulative Gross Protein Shake Sales (excluding GST/other taxes) through the Machine,
whichever occurs first. This threshold and ratio are also recorded in Schedule B and Schedule C, which shall be read consistently with this Clause 6. Where more than one Machine is placed at the same Gym premises (whether under this Agreement or a related agreement), the Milestone shall be tracked separately for each Machine unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

6.4 **Cup count methodology.** The cumulative cup count and sales figure shall be determined primarily from the Machine's transaction and dispensing records. The following shall generally not count toward the Milestone or toward Gross Protein Shake Sales:
- cancelled, refunded, or failed transactions;
- failed dispensing;
- test shakes or internal testing; and
- complimentary/free shakes or other transactions not representing a completed paid customer sale.

6.5 Relocation of the Machine does not reset the cumulative cup count, cumulative sales figure, or Milestone progress (see Clause 21.5).

6.6 **Gym audit right.** MuscleBoxPro shall, upon written request no more than once per calendar quarter, provide the Gym a transaction-level extract (cup count, gross sale value, and applicable exclusions under Clause 6.4) covering the requested period within 10 business days. If the Gym disputes the figures in good faith with reasonable supporting basis, the Parties shall reconcile within 15 business days; unresolved discrepancies affecting more than 2% of the disputed period's Gross Protein Shake Sales shall be escalated per Clause 46.

6.7 **Retail pricing.** MuscleBoxPro shall have sole discretion to set and revise the retail selling price of protein shakes dispensed through the Machine. MuscleBoxPro shall provide the Gym at least 7 days' prior written notice of any single price revision exceeding 10%.

### 7. NET PROFIT CALCULATION

**Net Profit = Gross Customer Sales − Applicable Taxes − Agreed Direct Variable Costs**

7.1 Agreed direct variable costs may include: ingredient cost; cup/packaging cost; payment gateway/transaction charges; refunds; promotional discounts where applicable; and other direct variable costs expressly agreed by the Parties in writing.

7.2 **Taxes.** Gross Customer Sales shall be considered exclusive of applicable GST or other indirect taxes collected on behalf of the Government for purposes of calculating Net Profit. Taxes collected from customers shall not constitute distributable profit.

7.3 **Excluded costs.** Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, the following shall not be deducted while calculating Net Profit: general corporate overhead; employee salaries of MuscleBoxPro; depreciation; financing costs; general marketing expenditure; general administrative expenses; and other general business expenses.

7.4 **Cost schedule.** MuscleBoxPro may maintain an agreed cost schedule for ingredients, cups, packaging, and other direct variable costs. Material changes to the agreed methodology shall be communicated to the Gym in writing at least 15 days in advance and documented as an amendment to Schedule C.

### 8. MONTHLY SETTLEMENT

8.1 Profit-sharing shall be calculated on a monthly basis.

8.2 At the end of each calendar month, MuscleBoxPro shall calculate Gross Customer Sales, applicable taxes, direct variable costs, Net Profit, the applicable profit-sharing ratio, the Gym's share of Net Profit, advertising revenue, the Gym's advertising share, electricity reimbursement, and applicable adjustments.

8.3 The Gym's net amount shall be settled within 15 days after the end of the relevant month, subject to completion of transaction reconciliation and availability of complete payment records.

8.4 Any demonstrable discrepancy shall be reviewed by the Parties in good faith per Clause 6.6.

8.5 **Late settlement.** If MuscleBoxPro fails to settle an undisputed amount within the 15-day window under Clause 8.3, simple interest at 12% per annum shall accrue on the overdue undisputed amount from the due date until paid.

8.6 **Withholding tax.** Each Party shall be responsible for its own tax deduction at source (TDS) obligations, if any, applicable to payments made under this Agreement in accordance with the Income-tax Act, 1961, and shall furnish the other Party the relevant withholding tax certificate (e.g., Form 16A) within the timelines prescribed by law where tax has been deducted at source.

### 10. ELECTRICITY REIMBURSEMENT

10.1 MuscleBoxPro shall provide the Gym an electricity reimbursement calculated at ₹1,000 for every completed 1,000 paid cups sold.

10.2 For each three-month review period, a minimum electricity reimbursement of ₹1,000 shall apply even if fewer than 1,000 cups are sold during that period.

10.3 The calculation shall therefore be:

| Cups sold in 3-month period | Electricity reimbursement |
|---|---|
| 0–999 | ₹1,000 |
| 1,000–1,999 | ₹1,000 |
| 2,000–2,999 | ₹2,000 |
| 3,000–3,999 | ₹3,000 |
| 4,000–4,999 | ₹4,000 |

10.4 Only completed blocks of 1,000 paid cups shall generate additional ₹1,000 reimbursement.

10.5 The minimum ₹1,000 reimbursement shall apply once per three-month review period.

10.6 Unused electricity reimbursement shall not carry forward to a subsequent three-month period.

10.7 Electricity reimbursement shall be settled as part of the applicable commercial reconciliation.

### 12. PERFORMANCE REVIEW

12.1 MuscleBoxPro shall review Machine performance every three months.

12.2 The review may consider cups sold, average monthly sales, revenue, Net Profit, Machine utilization, ingredient consumption, maintenance requirements, customer usage, operational problems, and overall commercial viability.

12.3 MuscleBoxPro may determine whether continued placement of the Machine at the Gym is commercially viable.

12.4 If MuscleBoxPro determines that the Machine is commercially underperforming, MuscleBoxPro may remove and redeploy the Machine by providing the Gym 15 days' written notice, provided that no removal under this Clause 12 shall take effect within the first 6 months from the Installation Date, so as to give the placement a fair initial run.

12.5 Removal for commercial underperformance shall not affect MuscleBoxPro's ownership of the Machine or the Gym's obligation to settle outstanding amounts, and no early-termination charge under Clause 36.2 shall apply to the Gym in this case.

12.6 **Reciprocal review right (Gym).** If, in any two consecutive three-month review periods, MuscleBoxPro (a) fails to meet the uptime commitment in Clause 19.4, or (b) fails to replenish ingredients/water within 3 business days of a Gym-reported stock-out on two or more occasions, the Gym may request removal of the Machine on 15 days' written notice. ## SCHEDULE C — PROFIT CALCULATION

**Step 1 — Gross Customer Sales:** Total amount received from completed paid customer transactions, excluding GST/other taxes collected on behalf of the Government.

**Step 2 — Deduct Direct Variable Costs:** Ingredient cost; cup/packaging cost; payment processing charges; refunds; applicable promotional discounts; and other specifically agreed direct variable costs.

**Step 3 — Net Profit:** Gross Sales − Direct Variable Costs.

**Step 4 — Profit Sharing:**
- Until the Milestone (15,000 cups **or** ₹5,00,000 cumulative Gross Protein Shake Sales, whichever first) is reached: **80% MuscleBoxPro / 20% Gym**.
- From the Milestone onward: **50% MuscleBoxPro / 50% Gym**.

**Advertising:** 80% MuscleBoxPro / 20% Gym (Milestone does not affect this ratio).

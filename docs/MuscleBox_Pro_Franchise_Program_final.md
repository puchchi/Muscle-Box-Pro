# MuscleBoxPro Franchise Program

## Build and Operate a MuscleBoxPro Network

MuscleBoxPro is building a technology-enabled network of automated protein shake machines across gyms and fitness centers.

The MuscleBoxPro Franchise Program allows selected partners to develop and operate a defined market while benefiting from the MuscleBoxPro technology platform, machine infrastructure, protein supply pipeline, centralized payment systems, advertising network, and established gym partnership model.

The franchisee invests in the expansion of the MuscleBoxPro network and participates in the profits generated by the machines assigned to their franchise.

# 14. Franchise Profit Model

The franchisee participates in the **profit generated by the MuscleBoxPro machines assigned to their franchise**, calculated using the single waterfall set out in Sections 15 and 16. The gym's profit share (Section 15) is deducted before the franchisee's Distributable Profit (Section 16) is calculated, so the same revenue is never counted toward both shares.

---

# 15. Gym-Level Profit Calculation

For each Machine, MuscleBoxPro will first calculate Gross Machine Profit as Gross Machine Revenue less:

- Protein cost
- Cup and consumable cost

The gym's profit share is calculated by applying the applicable gym-sharing percentage (see Section 11) to Gross Machine Profit, and is paid to the gym in accordance with the applicable gym agreement. This amount is deducted in Section 16 before the franchisee's Distributable Profit is calculated.

---

# 16. Franchise-Level Profit Calculation

The franchisee's **Distributable Profit** is calculated as Gross Machine Revenue, less the following costs, each only to the extent actually incurred and evidenced by supporting records:

- Protein cost
- Cup and consumable cost
- The gym's profit share payable under Section 15
- Payment processing charges
- Product wastage or spoilage actually incurred at the machine level
- MuscleBoxPro's own central logistics costs: transportation, warehousing, loading and unloading, and machine movement between MuscleBoxPro's central facilities and the franchisee's warehouse

This list is closed. It does **not** include the franchisee's own local operational costs under Section 27 (Local Operations) and Section 39 (Franchisee Responsibilities), which the franchisee bears directly and which are not deducted again here. A franchisee may query any deduction under Section 62 (Profit Calculation, Reporting and Audit Procedure).

# 17. Capital Recovery Model

The Territory Franchise operates under a capital recovery model.

Until the franchisee has received cumulative eligible protein-business profit equal to the Capital Recovery Threshold (₹29,50,000 at 18% GST; see Section 57):

## Protein Business

**100% of applicable MuscleBoxPro distributable protein-business profit is allocated to the franchisee.**

This continues until the franchisee has recovered the full Capital Recovery Threshold through eligible protein-business profit distributions.

---

# 18. Advertising Revenue

Advertising is treated separately from the capital recovery mechanism.

Advertising income does **not** count toward the franchisee's Capital Recovery Threshold.

### Advertising Profit Share

**25%: Franchisee**

**75%: MuscleBoxPro**

This advertising participation continues after the franchisee completes capital recovery, subject to the applicable advertising terms.

Advertising profit will be calculated after applicable advertising-related costs and expenses.

---

# 19. After Capital Recovery

Once the franchisee has received cumulative eligible protein-business profit equal to the Capital Recovery Threshold, the franchise moves into the post-recovery phase.

## Protein Business

**50%: Franchisee**

**50%: MuscleBoxPro**

## Advertising

**25%: Franchisee**

**75%: MuscleBoxPro**

The franchisee therefore continues to participate in the long-term economics of the MuscleBoxPro network even after recovering the initial franchise investment.

---

# 20. Capital Recovery Example

This example illustrates the calculation mechanism only. It is not a projection or forecast of actual revenue, profit, or timeline, and actual figures will depend on the factors listed in Section 56.

For a Territory Franchise, the Capital Recovery Threshold is ₹29,50,000 (₹25,00,000 base investment plus 18% GST; see Section 57).

If the franchisee has received:

**₹22,00,000**

in eligible protein-business profit distributions:

**Remaining recovery amount = ₹7,50,000**

Any advertising income received during this period does not reduce the ₹7,50,000 remaining recovery amount.

If the franchisee subsequently receives ₹10,00,000 in eligible protein-business profit:

- ₹7,50,000 completes capital recovery.
- The remaining ₹2,50,000 is distributed under the post-recovery 50:50 protein profit-sharing model.

# 58. Capital Protection After 24 Months

This Section 58 (including the Buy-In Option below) protects a franchisee whose franchise ends through no fault of its own. It does not apply, regardless of how much time has elapsed, where:

- MuscleBoxPro terminates the franchise for any ground listed in Section 45 (Termination), including material breach, fraud, misuse of Machines, unauthorized technology use, unauthorized product substitution, brand misuse, non-payment, serious operational non-compliance, failure to meet minimum requirements, unauthorized transfer, material misrepresentation, or conduct materially damaging to the MuscleBoxPro brand;
- MuscleBoxPro declines to renew the franchise term under Section 68 because the franchisee failed to meet the performance, compliance, or payment requirements under Sections 31 and 49; or
- the franchisee voluntarily exits the franchise under Section 44 for its own reasons.

In each of these cases, the Machines remain MuscleBoxPro's property, and no transfer, refund, or buy-in is available under this Section 58.

**This Section 58 applies** where, after the franchisee has completed 24 months from the Operational Start Date, and before the franchisee has reached the applicable Capital Recovery Threshold under Section 17 or Section 69:

- MuscleBoxPro terminates the franchise for its own business reasons, other than any ground listed in Section 45; or
- MuscleBoxPro declines to renew the franchise term under Section 68 for reasons unrelated to the franchisee's performance, compliance, or payment; or
- the Parties end the franchise by mutual written agreement that expressly states this Section 58 applies.

In any of these qualifying cases, MuscleBoxPro will notify the franchisee of its election between the following two options within **30 days** of the triggering event, and will, at its sole discretion, elect one of them:

- **Option 1, Machine Transfer:** Transfer ownership of the Machines allocated to the franchisee's Territory to the franchisee, in lieu of any cash refund of unrecovered capital; or
- **Option 2, Cash Refund:** Refund the remaining unrecovered capital amount (i.e., the applicable Capital Recovery Threshold less cumulative Distributable Profit already received by the franchisee) directly to the franchisee, within **30 days** of that election, together with interest at **18% per annum** from the triggering event until payment.

Where Option 1 applies:

- This transfer is conditional on the franchisee having no outstanding payment obligations to MuscleBoxPro at the time of transfer.
- Any GST, stamp duty, or other statutory cost arising from the transfer of Machine ownership will be borne by **the franchisee**.
- Following transfer, the franchisee may continue to operate the transferred Machines, but will no longer be entitled to use the MuscleBoxPro brand, technology, protein supply, payment infrastructure, or dashboard unless a separate commercial arrangement is agreed. **The Parties acknowledge that a transferred Machine will need to be reconfigured for independent, non-networked operation before it can be used**, and MuscleBoxPro will provide reasonable technical information to enable this at the franchisee's cost.

Where Option 2 applies, the franchise ends in the ordinary course and the Machines are returned to MuscleBoxPro in accordance with Section 44 (Franchise Exit).

## Buy-In Option for a Qualifying Termination Within the First 24 Months

If a termination that qualifies under this Section 58 (i.e., one within the categories above, not one of the excluded fault-based cases) occurs within the first 24 months from the Operational Start Date, Options 1 and 2 above are not available. Instead, in lieu of forfeiting its investment outright, the franchisee may propose a replacement party (an **"Incoming Party"**) to buy into the franchise in its place:

- The Incoming Party would pay the outgoing franchisee an amount towards the outgoing franchisee's remaining/unrecovered investment, as commercially negotiated between the outgoing franchisee and the Incoming Party.
- The Incoming Party would take over the franchise rights and obligations, functioning as a franchise transfer under Section 43 (Franchise Transfer).
- The Incoming Party must satisfy MuscleBoxPro's usual financial, operational, compliance, and brand requirements for a franchise transfer under Section 43.
- MuscleBoxPro will evaluate a complete Incoming Party proposal within **15 business days** and will not unreasonably withhold approval where the Incoming Party meets the requirements of this Section 58. **MuscleBoxPro's decision on a specific Incoming Party is final, and the franchisee cannot compel MuscleBoxPro to approve a particular Incoming Party.** This does not limit the franchisee's right to refer a dispute about whether MuscleBoxPro complied with the "will not unreasonably withhold approval" standard in this Section 58 to arbitration under Section 50, but the remedy for a proven breach of that standard is limited to damages, not an order compelling approval of any Incoming Party. MuscleBoxPro will provide written reasons if it declines a proposed Incoming Party.
- If no Incoming Party is approved within **60 days** of the termination taking effect, the Machines are returned to MuscleBoxPro in accordance with Section 44 (Franchise Exit), and MuscleBoxPro will not refund any unrecovered capital.

A termination within the first 24 months that falls within any of the fault-based exclusions at the start of this Section 58 (Section 45 grounds, performance-based non-renewal, or a voluntary exit under Section 44) does not qualify for the Buy-In Option either; in such cases, the Machines remain MuscleBoxPro's property, and no transfer, refund, or buy-in is available, regardless of how much time has elapsed.
